Getting Started

Create an account (with a custom emoji + color avatar) or log in, then pick your look from the 🎨 Skins button — four free skins, no cost, just equip and go. Hit Play to drop into the arena.

Controls & Movement

  • W A S D — move, Space — jump, Mouse — free look (click to lock your cursor in)
  • Left-click — punch, chop trees, harvest seagrass, or equip armor if you're holding a piece
  • Right-click — context-sensitive: eat held food, place logs, feed a tameable dino, or mount a tamed one
  • Shift + Right-click — tell a tamed dino to stay (click again to have it follow you)
  • Shift (alone) — dismount whatever you're riding
  • Scroll wheel — cycle through your hotbar
  • G — open the shop when you're near the hut
  • Esc — release your cursor; also opens a mouse sensitivity slider

Your hotbar starts empty — slots only appear as you actually pick things up.

Health & Breathing

You have 17 hearts. Watch the 5 breathing bubbles under your health bar whenever you're underwater — you lose one bubble every 1.5 seconds submerged, and once they're gone you take 1.5 hearts/second until you surface or drown. Riding a tamed underwater dino grants unlimited underwater breathing.

Combat

Punch dinosaurs and trees by aiming and left-clicking. Dinosaurs always have to turn and face you before they can chase or hit back, so circling a slow one can buy you time. Getting hit knocks you back and gives you a brief invulnerability window.

Gear that helps:

  • Axe (4 logs) — fells trees in one hit, boosts fiber drops from 50% to 75%
  • Sword (5 shells + 3 logs) — deals +1 heart of damage to dinosaurs
  • Dino Shell Tunic (10 shells) — −25% damage taken
  • Dino Shell Leg Piece (6 shells) — −15%
  • Dino Shell Helmet (6 shells) — −15%
  • Wear all three for a 70% total reduction (there's a bonus for the full set)

Eating: select meat or cutlets and right-click — meat restores 1 heart, cutlets restore half a heart.

Placing logs: select logs and right-click to drop a waist-high pile; a second log on the same spot stacks taller than you and blocks dinosaurs from walking through it.

Trees Grow Over Time

The forest isn't static — trees start as practically invisible seeds, grow into small plants, then miniature trees, and finally full trees over a few minutes each stage. Only full-grown trees can be chopped.

Taming & Riding

Right-click an eligible dino to feed it — it'll use whatever you're carrying. Once tamed, right-click again to ride; press Shift to dismount. Any tamed dino can be told to stay or follow with Shift + right-click.

Exploring & Key Locations

  • The world is bounded by an invisible wall roughly 195 units from center
  • Rivers wind through the map — good hunting ground for seagrass and underwater dinos
  • A mountain range holds the shop: a strange glowing hut where the ground slopes down in the center of the range — press G nearby to buy gear
  • A small camp (tents, crates, a campfire) sits near your spawn point

Strategy Tips

  • Tame a raptor early — the speed boost makes resource runs much faster
  • Buy the axe first (makes chopping trivial and boosts fiber), then armor as shells allow
  • A tamed sauropod is the best investment for late-game survival
  • Don't dive deep without a bubble buffer or a tamed aquatic mount
  • Placed log barricades are a cheap, renewable way to block aggressive dinos
